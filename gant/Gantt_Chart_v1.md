## Project Research Questions

> **What is the cause of the disease from the gene, protein, structure and function perspectives?**
> * What are the differences at gene, protein, structure and function levels between a reference/healthy state and the disease-associated state?
> * From a biological perspective, how are the differences between reference/healthy state and disease-state impacting the patient’s function?
> * What are the differences of the gene, protein, structure and function between humans and at least 2 other relevant species? What biological implications does this have?
